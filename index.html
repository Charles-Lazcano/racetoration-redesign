02 · Cleaning

Dry Ice & Laser Cleaning

Dry Ice Blasting is a deep cleaning process that combines kinetic energy and thermal shock. Once solid CO2 pellets are projected from a powerful air nozzle, they sublimate at contact with a surface and create micro-explosions. This process in turn releases foreign debris from the substrate without abrasion, and brings the material back to where it began.

For the automotive lovers, this is a game changer. Restore your vehicle to factory finishes or prep for the next step in building. Restore engine bays without spraying cans of shine product to make it look cheaper than it actually is. Prep undercarriage for coatings or rust prevention. Clean those pesky hard to reach crevasses in wiring and interiors.
